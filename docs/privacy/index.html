Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Silicon: Tech Tycoon is built privacy-first. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising. Your game stays on your device; we process purchase information and anonymous paywall views to restore access and evaluate subscription pricing.

The short version

Your game save stays on your device. There is no account, no login, no tracking, and no ads. On iOS, RevenueCat processes an anonymous installation identifier and purchase status to validate and restore access. This service starts when the app opens, including when you have not bought anything.

What data do we collect?

When a purchase screen is shown, RevenueCat also receives a paywall-view event and the displayed offering identifier, associated with the anonymous installation identifier. We use these events and purchase outcomes to compare pricing variants and improve our purchase screen. These analytics do not include your game save or gameplay activity.

The purchase service processes purchase history and status and an anonymous identifier that RevenueCat generates for your installation so access can be validated and restored.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, browsing activity, or your game saves or gameplay activity. We do not collect crash reports. The anonymous identifier is created by the app; it is not your Apple ID, your advertising identifier, or anything that identifies you as a person. None of it is used for tracking, none of it is sold, and none of it is combined with data from other apps or companies.

The purchase service initializes when the iOS app starts. RevenueCat can process the anonymous installation identifier and check purchase status even if you do not buy anything.

Where is my game data stored?

Your save game is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (localStorage on web; via Capacitor on iOS). It never leaves your device and is not synced to any cloud service.

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data will erase the save. Use the Export save option in Settings to create a backup.

Third-party SDKs and services

There are no advertising networks, crash reporters or social-login libraries. RevenueCat provides purchase processing and paywall analytics. The third-party code in the app is:

  • Capacitor — native iOS packaging. Sends nothing.
  • Apple's StoreKit — takes the payment. Apple holds the purchase record under Apple's own policy.
  • RevenueCat — validates purchases and makes them restorable across reinstalls. It receives your purchase history and the anonymous installation identifier described above, and acts as our data processor. It also records purchase-screen views and pricing-offering assignments for the analytics described above; gameplay activity is not sent.
  • Founding Owner access in version 1.4.0 is checked locally using Apple's verified original purchase. This version no longer sends the original app receipt to our former verification endpoint. Earlier versions may still attempt that request; the endpoint is unavailable.

In-app purchases

The app is free to download. Silicon Pro is available as an auto-renewing subscription (weekly or yearly, with a free trial for eligible accounts) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Existing monthly subscriptions and the legacy one-time Creative Mode unlock remain supported.

We never see your payment details. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit; card numbers and billing information never reach us or our processor. What we do receive is the fact of the purchase — which product, and whether it is still active — so the app knows what to unlock and can restore it if you reinstall or switch devices. Purchase records are also held by Apple under their Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends; you can manage or cancel one at any time in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children's privacy

Silicon is rated 4+ and is not directed at children specifically, but it is safe for them: there is no tracking, no advertising, no profiling, and no way for anyone to contact anyone else through the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, and the purchase data described above contains none.

Changes to this policy

We update the date above when this policy changes. The latest update explains anonymous paywall analytics for pricing experiments and the weekly subscription option in version 1.4.0.
