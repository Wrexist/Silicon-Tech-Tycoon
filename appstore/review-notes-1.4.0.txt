Silicon: Tech Tycoon 1.4.0

ACCESS
No app account or login is required. Start a company on first launch; optional coach tips can be skipped. Core gameplay and saves work offline. Store purchases and restore require Apple's purchase services.

PURCHASES
Open Settings using the gear button, then the Silicon Pro section to view the single purchase screen. It offers weekly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions and a separate one-time lifetime unlock. Prices and eligible trial terms are loaded from the store. Both subscriptions currently have a one-week introductory trial for eligible customers; lifetime does not renew.

Existing monthly subscribers retain access and can restore their purchases; monthly is no longer listed for new purchases.

Restore purchases is available in Settings and on the purchase screen. Active subscribers can open Manage subscription from Settings. The previously sold Creative Mode non-consumable remains restorable for existing owners; it is not offered as a new purchase.

The free game is playable without a purchase. Silicon Pro unlocks additional content and modes described on the purchase screen. TestFlight/sandbox installs do not automatically receive the historical Founding Owner grant. That grant requires a verified production original download from the paid-app era.

VERSION 1.4.0
Refreshed offices and robot staff, improved factory placement and touch controls, saved device drafts, and improvements to save recovery and phone/tablet navigation. Supports iPhone portrait and all four iPad orientations.

No tracking, advertising, social login or user-to-user messaging. Purchases are processed by StoreKit and RevenueCat. Game progress stays on the device and can be exported from Settings.
