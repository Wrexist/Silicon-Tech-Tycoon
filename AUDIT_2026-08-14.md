# Game Audit — 2026-08-14

Five audits (noise, home-screen structure, guidance, feature surface, architecture) run against
`main` @ f02288c. Fixes shipped on `claude/game-audits-cleanup-ner88f` are marked ✅; everything else
is left open with a recommendation, because narrowing the game's scope is a design call, not a
cleanup call.

Baseline: 158 test files / 1,654 tests green. After this branch: 160 / 1,670, determinism pin
untouched.

## 1. Noise audit — what talks over the player

The scaffolding from the July round is holding well (Decision Inbox triage, 4/3-week interrupt gap,
BuzzTicker at 9s, GainFX passive-token throttle, toast cap of 3). The remaining noise had moved from
the *interrupt* layer to the *ambient* layer — toasts and dots, which no budget governs.

| # | Offender | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Toast cap evicted the OLDEST line, so a passive "Revenue milestone, $10M!" could push the answer to the player's own tap off screen mid-read | ✅ toasts carry a `priority`; `"low"` lines are dropped rather than shown when full, and are evicted first |
| 2 | Fan + revenue milestones fired **once per threshold crossed** — a fast-forward vaulting three tiers was three toasts | ✅ only the highest threshold crossed per tick is announced, at low priority |
| 3 | A week retiring several products emitted one toast **each** | ✅ coalesced to one line naming the biggest earner (`+N more`), toned by *its* verdict |
| 4 | Research completion fired two toasts for one event (completion + "Design budget +N EP") | ✅ folded into a single line when both land on the same tick |
| 5 | `hq` nav dot carried ~8 unrelated meanings in one identical red dot — always lit by mid-game, i.e. meaningless (carried over from July, item 6) | ✅ split into two weights: `"act"` (an answer is owed and expires) vs `"opportunity"` (available whenever) |
| 6 | 135 `showToast` call sites overall; ~25 in FactoryMode alone | Open — the priority seam now exists, so the sweep is mechanical: audit each site for "does this echo something already visible?" |
| 7 | 20+ interrupt overlays mounted unconditionally in `AppShell`'s tail | Open — cosmetic, each self-gates and returns null, but the mount list is now the least readable part of `App.tsx` |

**Worst-case tick before this branch:** ~8 toasts (fan milestone + revenue milestone + 3 product
finishes + research + design budget + staff levels), staggered over ~4s by the 600/700/800/1400ms
defer offsets, through a 3-slot window that silently dropped whichever the player was reading.
**After:** at most 4, with the passive ones yielding.

## 2. Home-screen structure audit — the 20-card scroll

HQ is the screen every session starts on, and it had grown to **~27 independently-gated cards in one
flat, unlabelled column**: office scene → ready → choice → poach → buzz → scenario → challenge →
daily → IPO → era → stats → runway → ladder → nemesis → era goal → unlock → next move → contracts →
legacy → live ops → community → team focus → production → upgrades → performance → insights → news.

Nothing told the player which zone they were in, so the scroll had no shape.

✅ **Shipped:** the stream is grouped into three labelled zones behind an unlabelled priority band.

| Zone | Contains | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| *(top, unlabelled)* | ready-to-launch, event choice, poach, IPO, era advance, run trackers | Wants an answer **now** |
| **Your company** | stats, runway, rank ladder, nemesis duel, era goal, next move, contracts | Where you **stand** |
| **Operations** | Legacy Era, live ops, in-production, team focus, community, office upgrades | How the business **runs** |
| **Records** | performance, news | What **happened** |

The group wrapper reproduces `.hq`'s own column + gap, so no card moved a pixel — the only new
ink is a hairline label. Labels hide themselves over an empty group via `:has()` (verified surviving
minification in `dist/`), so an early-game player never sees an "Operations" heading over blank
space.

**Open:** the priority band can still stack five cards at once (ready + choice + poach + IPO + era).
In practice those rarely co-occur, but a hard "one decision at a time" rule at the top would be the
next win.

## 3. Guidance audit — four voices telling you what to do

The single clearest clarity problem, and the largest fix on this branch.

A post-tutorial player was being directed by **four systems at once**, in four voices, with no
hierarchy and no dedupe between them:

1. `NextMoveCard` — the authored objective ladder (1 directive), high in the scroll
2. `StrategicInsightsCard` — up to 3 advisory hints, **ten cards further down**
3. `ContractsCard` — 2–3 live goals
4. `navAttention` — dots on up to 5 tabs

Nothing stopped 1 and 2 saying the same thing. With the objective "Buy an office upgrade" active,
the insights card would independently surface "Your Amenities can be upgraded to … for $X" — the same
instruction, different wording, ten rows apart.

✅ **Shipped:**
- The two cards are **merged into one**. Primary = the objective (step counter, why, deep-link);
  secondary = at most **2** hints under an "Also worth doing" rule.
- Hints the active objective already states are **dropped** (`OBJECTIVE_SUBSUMES`, 7 objectives
  mapped to 19 hint ids, integrity-tested both ways so a rename can't silently turn a rule into a
  no-op).
- Hints collect **4 deep** and show 2, so deduping one away doesn't shrink the card.
- When the ladder is complete the top hint is **promoted into the primary slot** — replacing the old
  content-free "All set-up goals complete / You're running the show now" placard, which occupied a
  full card to say nothing.

Net: **4 directives in 2 cards → 3 in 1 card**, with the redundant ones removed rather than reworded.

**Open:** the contract board is still a third goal system alongside the ladder. It earns its place
(claimable rewards, regenerating), but "Contracts" and "Next move" reading as peers is worth a
design pass.

## 4. Feature surface audit — add / remove / change

78 engine modules back the game. The systems are individually good; the problem is **discovery
density**, not quality. Findings, none shipped — each is a design call:

**Consider removing / demoting**
- **`GetStartedCard`** duplicates the `Coach` almost exactly (both walk design → build → launch, both
  pre-first-ship). It only renders when `tutorialDone` and nothing is launched — i.e. for a player who
  *skipped* the coach. Defensible, but two authored copies of one checklist is a maintenance trap.
- **`BoardConfidenceStrip`, `NemesisDuelCard`, `CommunityCard`, `TeamFocusStrip`** are four separate
  always-on strips on HQ, each surfacing one number. Strong candidates for one "Company pulse" row.
- **Milestone feed lines** (revenue/fan thresholds) are already classified `"low"` salience and hidden
  behind the News card's collapse. They now also toast at low priority. That's two mitigations for
  content that could simply fire less often.

**Consider changing**
- **`Upgrades` sits under "Operations"** but is really a shop. If the office shop grows further it
  wants its own sheet, not a card in the home scroll.
- **The Records zone is read-only** — Performance + News. That's a natural candidate for the Progress
  sheet, which would take two more cards off the home screen entirely.

**Consider adding**
- Nothing. The game does not need more systems; five of the eight findings above are about surfacing
  what already exists more calmly.
