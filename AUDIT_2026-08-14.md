# Game Audit — 2026-08-14

Five audits (noise, home-screen structure, guidance, feature surface, architecture) run against
`main` @ f02288c. Fixes shipped on `claude/game-audits-cleanup-ner88f` are marked ✅; everything else
is left open with a recommendation, because narrowing the game's scope is a design call, not a
cleanup call.

Baseline: 158 test files / 1,654 tests green. After this branch: 160 / 1,670, determinism pin
untouched.

---

## 1. Noise audit — what talks over the player

The scaffolding from the July round is holding well (Decision Inbox triage, 4/3-week interrupt gap,
BuzzTicker at 9s, GainFX passive-token throttle, toast cap of 3). The remaining noise had moved from
the *interrupt* layer to the *ambient* layer — toasts and dots, which no budget governs.

| # | Offender | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Toast cap evicted the OLDEST line, so a passive "Revenue milestone, $10M!" could push the answer to the player's own tap off screen mid-read | ✅ toasts carry a `priority`; `"low"` lines are dropped rather than shown when full, and are evicted first |
| 2 | Fan + revenue milestones fired **once per threshold crossed** — a fast-forward vaulting three tiers was three toasts | ✅ only the highest threshold crossed per tick is announced, at low priority |
| 3 | A week retiring several products emitted one toast **each** | ✅ coalesced to one line naming the biggest earner (`+N more`), toned by *its* verdict |
| 4 | Research completion fired two toasts for one event (completion + "Design budget +N EP") | ✅ folded into a single line when both land on the same tick |
| 5 | `hq` nav dot carried ~8 unrelated meanings in one identical red dot — always lit by mid-game, i.e. meaningless (carried over from July, item 6) | ✅ split into two weights: `"act"` (an answer is owed and expires) vs `"opportunity"` (available whenever) |
| 6 | 135 `showToast` call sites — audited each for "does this echo something already on screen?" | ✅ four pure echoes cut (machine placed, undo, OS philosophy, staff let go). The other 131 survive the test on purpose: they are refusal *reasons* (which explain a rejected tap and are the opposite of noise) or outcomes with no visible surface |
| 7 | 20+ interrupt overlays mounted unconditionally in `AppShell`'s tail | ✅ gathered into `components/Interrupts.tsx`, ordered to match `interruptPriority.ts` so the mount list and the priority list can be read against each other |

**Worst-case tick before this branch:** ~8 toasts (fan milestone + revenue milestone + 3 product
finishes + research + design budget + staff levels), staggered over ~4s by the 600/700/800/1400ms
defer offsets, through a 3-slot window that silently dropped whichever the player was reading.
**After:** at most 4, with the passive ones yielding.

## 2. Home-screen structure audit — the 20-card scroll

HQ is the screen every session starts on, and it had grown to **~27 independently-gated cards in one
flat, unlabelled column**: office scene → ready → choice → poach → buzz → scenario → challenge →
daily → IPO → era → stats → runway → ladder → nemesis → era goal → unlock → next move → contracts →
legacy → live ops → community → team focus → production → upgrades → performance → insights → news.

Nothing told the player which zone they were in, so the scroll had no shape.

✅ **Shipped:** the stream is grouped into three labelled zones behind an unlabelled priority band.

| Zone | Contains | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| *(top, unlabelled)* | ready-to-launch, event choice, poach, IPO, era advance, run trackers | Wants an answer **now** |
| **Your company** | stats, runway, rank ladder, nemesis duel, era goal, next move, contracts | Where you **stand** |
| **Operations** | Legacy Era, live ops, in-production, team focus, community, office upgrades | How the business **runs** |
| **Records** | performance, news | What **happened** |

The group wrapper reproduces `.hq`'s own column + gap, so no card moved a pixel — the only new
ink is a hairline label. Labels hide themselves over an empty group via `:has()` (verified surviving
minification in `dist/`), so an early-game player never sees an "Operations" heading over blank
space.

✅ **The priority band now holds one call-to-action per kind.** It could stack five (ready + choice +
poach + IPO + era), which is the same as the band meaning nothing.
- An event choice and a poach attempt could both be open: the event leads, the poach appears the
  instant it's answered. Neither expires, so nothing is lost.
- IPO and era-advance are guarded to one. Today the balance makes that automatic — IPO needs era 4,
  and era 4→5 needs `wentPublic` — but the invariant now lives in the band rather than resting on a
  constant two modules away.

✅ **The vital-signs row went from two rows of six pills to one row of four.** The second row existed
only because six didn't fit, and was negative-margined up to pretend it was the first. It also led
with trivia: "Products" duplicates the Performance card's own Shipped count and "Team" is both the
Company tab's entire subject and literally visible as desks in the office above. What's left —
Cash · Runway · Reputation · Era — is what the player steers by, money first.

## 3. Guidance audit — four voices telling you what to do

The single clearest clarity problem, and the largest fix on this branch.

A post-tutorial player was being directed by **four systems at once**, in four voices, with no
hierarchy and no dedupe between them:

1. `NextMoveCard` — the authored objective ladder (1 directive), high in the scroll
2. `StrategicInsightsCard` — up to 3 advisory hints, **ten cards further down**
3. `ContractsCard` — 2–3 live goals
4. `navAttention` — dots on up to 5 tabs

Nothing stopped 1 and 2 saying the same thing. With the objective "Buy an office upgrade" active,
the insights card would independently surface "Your Amenities can be upgraded to … for $X" — the same
instruction, different wording, ten rows apart.

✅ **Shipped:**
- The two cards are **merged into one**. Primary = the objective (step counter, why, deep-link);
  secondary = at most **2** hints under an "Also worth doing" rule.
- Hints the active objective already states are **dropped** (`OBJECTIVE_SUBSUMES`, 7 objectives
  mapped to 19 hint ids, integrity-tested both ways so a rename can't silently turn a rule into a
  no-op).
- Hints collect **4 deep** and show 2, so deduping one away doesn't shrink the card.
- When the ladder is complete the top hint is **promoted into the primary slot** — replacing the old
  content-free "All set-up goals complete / You're running the show now" placard, which occupied a
  full card to say nothing.

✅ **`GetStartedCard` is gone** — a *third* copy of the same guidance. It drew a Design → Build →
Launch checklist for players who skipped the Coach, but the objective ladder's first rung is that
checklist (with a progress bar and the same deep-link), and the Ready-to-launch / In-production cards
carry its other two steps live and actionable.

Net: **5 directives across 3 cards → 3 in 1 card**, with the redundant ones removed rather than
reworded.

**Open (design call, not cleanup):** the contract board is still a third goal system alongside the
ladder. It earns its place — claimable rewards, regenerating targets — but "Contracts" and "Next
move" reading as equal peers is worth a deliberate pass rather than a cleanup guess.

## 4. Feature surface audit — add / remove / change

78 engine modules back the game. The systems are individually good; the problem is **discovery
density**, not quality. Findings, none shipped — each is a design call:

**Removed** ✅
- **`GetStartedCard`** — the third copy of design → build → launch (see audit 3).
- **Two vital-signs pills** — Products and Team, both duplicated elsewhere on the same screen.

**Left alone, deliberately** — each is a design decision rather than a cleanup, and guessing at them
would cost more than the clutter does:
- **`NemesisDuelCard` + `CommunityCard` + `TeamFocusStrip`** look like candidates for one "company
  pulse" row, but they aren't the same kind of thing: two are readouts and `TeamFocusStrip` is an
  interactive control with real costs. Merging a lever into a dashboard makes it harder to find, not
  easier. (A correction to this document's first draft: `BoardConfidenceStrip` was listed here as a
  fourth top-level strip — it is not, it renders nested inside `LegacyEraCard`.)
- **`Upgrades` in the home scroll.** It's a shop and would be tidier in a sheet, but the objective
  ladder deep-links to it on the `hq` tab, and burying a core progression system to shorten a scroll
  is a trade the scroll doesn't warrant.
- **The Records zone** (Performance + News) could move into the Progress sheet, taking two more cards
  off home. But the Progress sheet is opened rarely, and "how is my company doing" is a question
  players ask constantly. It stays, last and under its own label, which is already out of the way.
- **Milestone feed lines** could simply fire less often at the engine level. They now have two
  mitigations (low salience in the News card, low toast priority), so the remaining pressure is on
  balance, not noise — and balance changes are the one thing this branch shouldn't smuggle in.

**Adding**
- Nothing. The game does not need more systems; every finding here is about surfacing what already
  exists more calmly.

| `screens/Market.tsx` held the same shape: 130 lines of authored post-mortem copy (`performanceDrivers`, `generateTips`, `verdictOf`) — pure functions of a `LaunchedProduct`, zero tests | ✅ moved into `engine/postmortem.ts` beside the ranking layer they belong to, as `launchDrivers` / `launchTips` / `verdictOf`. Market.tsx 2,012 → 1,877; 10 new tests, including the two rules that matter — never fabricate a number an old save doesn't carry, and always leave the player a next step |
| `App.tsx` mounted ~15 interrupt overlays as a bare wall of sibling tags | ✅ `components/Interrupts.tsx`, ordered to match `interruptPriority.ts`. App.tsx 1,009 → 983 |
| `state/` vs `engine/` layering | Healthy. `insights.ts` went to `state/` precisely because it reads `runwayWeeks`/`deskCapacity`/`upgradeCost` — engine must never import state for values. `postmortem.ts` stayed in `engine/` because it reads nothing but its argument |
| `DesignLab.tsx` (2,387), `Company.tsx` (1,635) | Open — same shape, and worth the same treatment when they're next opened for a feature. Not churned here: unlike the HQ and Market cases there was no *untested player-facing logic* to rescue, so the change would be movement without payoff |
| Determinism | Untouched. Every change on this branch is presentation-layer or a derived read; nothing writes `GameState` and the pin is green |
| Monetization boundary | Untouched. No engine file reads an entitlement; the paywall remains the single purchase surface. The era-advance card — the app's primary conversion moment — was checked against the new one-milestone-at-a-time rule and cannot be hidden by it (IPO needs era 4; the free tier caps at era 2) |
